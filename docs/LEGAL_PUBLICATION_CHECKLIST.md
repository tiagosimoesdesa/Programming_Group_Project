# Legal publication checklist

The in-app Terms & Conditions and Privacy notice describe the behaviour currently verified in this repository. They intentionally do not invent the following information. The team must obtain legal review and complete these items before treating the documents as publication-ready:

- Identify the legal person or natural person who operates COUNT THE JOLS and acts as data controller, including a service address and working privacy/legal contact.
- Decide whether the service is a student/non-commercial project or is offered by a trader. If consumer rules apply, add the required trader identification, complaint-book information and competent Portuguese alternative-dispute-resolution entity.
- Approve and implement a documented retention/deletion schedule for accounts, photos, submissions, competition history, login-attempt records, refresh sessions, provider logs and backups.
- Provide a practical account-deletion and GDPR-request process, including how shared-night photos and records are handled when one participant requests erasure.
- Confirm the production Supabase project region, Render/OpenAI contractual roles, subprocessor lists, international-transfer locations and safeguards, backup retention, and incident contacts.
- Decide whether a Data Protection Officer is required and, if one is appointed, publish the DPO contact details.
- Establish content reporting, moderation, takedown and appeal procedures appropriate to the service and assess whether the Digital Services Act applies to the operator’s actual activity.
- Obtain permission or licences for the app name, logos, photographs, beer imagery, fonts, source code and other materials, and add the correct rights-holder notices.
- Confirm the intended countries, minimum-age rule and whether stronger age assurance is required. The current checkbox is only self-attestation.
- Have Portuguese/EU counsel review governing-law, liability, consumer-protection, alcohol, privacy and platform terms before public launch.

Primary legal references considered for this draft include Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Portuguese Law No. 58/2019, Portuguese Law No. 41/2004, Decree-Law No. 50/2013 as amended by Decree-Law No. 106/2015, Directive 93/13/EEC, and Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 where applicable.
